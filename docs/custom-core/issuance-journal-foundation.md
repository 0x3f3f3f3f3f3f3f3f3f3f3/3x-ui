# Issuance journal foundation

This is the Task 3 control-plane primitive under [restore-authority-lifecycle-plan.md](restore-authority-lifecycle-plan.md). Explicit acceptance fixtures authorize business listeners through it; the ordinary managed bootstrap has not yet enabled it. Existing managed remote-scope guards remain in force.

`internal/policyauthority` explicitly creates or opens one private synchronous bbolt journal outside imported SQL and node execution state. Creation refuses an existing file; opening refuses missing, wrong-identity, shared-permission or contradictory state. Initial seeds remain immutable. Confirmed raw/billed history, initial and per-grant fractions, current window use, uncertain frozen allowance, outstanding finite capacity and directional rate/burst sums survive reopen. Opening recomputes cumulative history and held allocation sums from the complete records.

Issuance binds authority identity/generation, node/source/fresh boot, canonical client, window/version and request/challenge. A transaction commits the finite allocation before returning it. Exact request retries reuse the committed grant; changed intent is rejected. Limited zero directional shares are distinct from explicit unlimited mode. Independently issued capacity and limited rate/burst sums cannot exceed their policy bounds. Reported billed consumption is accumulated directly, without applying a multiplier again. Cumulative reports reject regression, conflicting sequence reuse and capacity excess.

A current-incarnation seal releases only verified unused capacity and its shares. Node reboot, expiry, lost control, deletion or an unconfirmed report releases nothing. Retired boot identities are durably recorded and cannot be reactivated by delayed registration. Deleted canonical identities retain terminal tombstones and old allocation/history. Exact provisioning retries preserve the original seed and current confirmed totals.

Schema 2 adds immutable policy/window transition records and a per-account revision. Exact change/reset retries preserve the first captured boundary, including after reopen and later reports. Reset deliberately credits confirmed period usage and initial frozen uncertainty, while retaining outstanding grants and lifetime evidence. Late reports after the boundary enter the current window. Policy decreases retain old quota/rate/burst holds; an old unlimited directional grant also blocks new limited allocation until sealed. A prior window identifier cannot be reused. Historical grants remain tied to their original policy.

Confirmed and held billed quantities use whole bytes plus millionths, with exact carry/borrow across grants, resets and reopen. Settlement never applies the multiplier again. Fractional usage cannot regress or exceed its finite grant, and sealing cannot round remaining capacity up. Every committed effect advances a signed-SQL-compatible revision once; retries do not. Opening recomputes revisions, policy history, cumulative fractions and remaining capacity. Earlier schema-1 development journals are rejected and retained for explicit reconciliation; missing history is never silently initialized.

The conservative allocator retains outstanding quota until an authenticated durable seal. Expiration alone does not reclaim billed capacity. A durably replaced boot cannot renew; its directional rate/burst shares may be released only after the maximum ten-second execution lease has elapsed on the authority process's monotonic clock. Reopening the authority restarts the full quarantine for unreleased retired shares. The journal records this retirement separately from a seal and recomputes its account revision and rate sums when reopened. Confirmed use, fractions and unsealed capacity remain held. This trades availability for exclusion; a private controller must renew or replace execution grants while retaining the authority's quota holds.

Configured core construction generates a fresh 128-bit boot nonce before opening recovered accounts or arming expiry callbacks. Capabilities add protobuf field 7 (`boot_id`) and advertise `boot-bound-execution-grants-v1` only on a configured persistent instance. Private Unix RPCs bind the authority, obtain monotonic challenges, install finite grants, checkpoint their cumulative exact usage and seal them. A grant matches authority/generation/node, source, boot, client, policy version and quota window. Installation persists its accounting boundary before enabling admission. Duplicates preserve the original deadline, capacity and limiter tokens; retired grant sequences cannot reactivate. Fresh boots do not activate stored grants or seal a previous incarnation's capacity.

Admission enforces the finite billed capacity and explicit directional shares, including unavailable zero shares. Lease loss or expiry closes applicable sessions and rejects reconnection. Fresh monotonic challenges can renew an existing grant without issuing capacity, repricing usage or refilling limiter tokens; exact renewal retries retain the original deadline. Expired or superseded grants cannot renew. An explicit durable handoff seals the old grant and pauses traffic until its replacement arrives; the old deadline still closes it on failure. Pending connection registrations also wait during this controlled handoff, limited to 128 per client and bounded by the old deadline/cancellation. Ordinary sealed or missing authority remains a rejection. Sealing commits final spent capacity and exact counters before acknowledging unused capacity. Storage failure exposes no successful seal and closes admission. TCP readers/writers and SSH reverse writers segment streams by the assigned burst and affordable remaining capacity; UDP packets retain their original boundary. The panel adapter requires the negotiated fresh boot and grant capabilities before sending these calls.

Its file lock excludes a second open of the same file; it cannot exclude a simultaneously operated copy of the whole authority or prevent authority-state rollback. Those cases require an external fencing source. Copied node SQL/execution snapshots must be fenced by fresh core incarnations and this unchanged authority; that integration remains open.
